Why choose PoopDood?

PoopDood provides reliable dog poop removal and pet waste cleanup services for busy dog owners in Lubbock and surrounding areas. We make it easy to keep your yard clean, sanitary, and safe for your family and pets without the hassle of scooping yourself.

Whether you need weekly pooper scooper service, a one-time dog waste cleanup, or routine pet waste removal, we’ve got you covered with simple pricing and easy online booking. We show up on time, clean thoroughly, and help keep your outdoor space looking its best year-round.

At PoopDood, our goal is simple: affordable, dependable dog poop cleanup with friendly local service you can trust. Let us handle the dirty work so you can enjoy a cleaner yard and more time with your dogs.

Do I do one-time pickups?

Absolutely! Whether it's before a backyard party, an event, or just when life's too busy — PoopDood offers quick, reliable one-time cleanups to make your yard fresh and ready to enjoy.
