5 Signs You Need a Professional Pooper Scooper

🗓 May 19, 2026 ✍️ Corbin Key ⏱ 3 min read
Share: 📘 Facebook

Most people wait too long before calling a professional pooper scooper. It's understandable — it feels like something you should just handle yourself. But there are some clear signs that the situation has gotten past the point of a quick DIY fix, or that your schedule just doesn't allow for it anymore.

1
You can smell the yard from inside

If you open a back window or walk out onto your porch and you're hit with a smell, your yard has accumulated enough waste that the ammonia and bacteria are off-gassing in a real way. At this point you're not just dealing with cosmetics — you've got an active odor and hygiene problem.

2
Your grass has dead brown patches

Dog waste contains high concentrations of nitrogen. In small amounts, nitrogen is good for grass. In concentrated amounts from repeated waste in the same spots, it chemically burns the lawn. Those dead patches are a classic sign of accumulated waste — and they won't recover until the waste is properly removed and the soil gets time to reset.

3
Flies are multiplying in your yard

Dog waste is one of the top breeding grounds for flies. If you've noticed a sudden increase in flies around your patio or back door, there's a good chance poop buildup in the yard is the source. This also creates problems for neighbors nearby.

4
You've avoided the yard for more than a week

This one's simple but honest: if you or your family have stopped using the backyard because of the state of it, that's the sign right there. Your yard should be a space you enjoy, not a minefield you avoid.

5
Cleanup keeps getting postponed

Life gets busy. Kids, work, weekends that fill up fast — it happens to everyone. If you've told yourself "I'll get to it this weekend" for three weekends in a row, a recurring service plan is the practical solution. It removes the task from your to-do list entirely so you never have to think about it.
