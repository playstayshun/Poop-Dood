🐾 What We Do

PoopDood provides professional dog poop removal and pet waste cleanup throughout Lubbock, TX and nearby neighborhoods. Our service is simple, reliable, and affordable — with flexible scheduling so it fits around your life, not the other way around.

We offer weekly, biweekly, twice-a-week, and one-time cleanups — no contracts, no hassle. You don't even need to be home. As long as your yard is accessible, we'll show up, clean it out, and text you when we're done. Every single time.

Our mission hasn't changed since day one: Your Dog, Our Mess.

👋 Who We Are

I'm Corbin, the founder of PoopDood. My dad suggested the idea — I think he found it online somewhere. I sat on it for about a year, not sure if it would actually work. Then one day I just got tired of being scared of hearing "no" and went for it. Turns out the worst thing that could happen was nothing.

I've been cleaning yards across Lubbock and surrounding neighborhoods for 5+ years. I got into it while doing landscaping and started asking customers if they wanted me to come back weekly. It grew from there. I'm not here to be wildly different from every other service out there — I just want to be the reliable person who shows up and gets it done.

Dog poop is actually bad for grass. It shouldn't just sit in your yard. That's the whole thing. Your Dog, Our Mess.
